Refund Policy

Butter Edit · Effective 16 November 2026

You can try Butter Edit free for 30 days before paying. Every feature is open during the trial, so you can test it with your own camera and your own photos before deciding.

1. Refund window

We refund any purchase within 14 days, no reason needed. Just ask.

2. How to ask

Email thefilmtravel@gmail.com with the email address you used at checkout or your order number. Your receipt email also links to a customer portal where you can see the order.

3. How it is processed

Payments and refunds are handled by our merchant of record, Dodo Payments. The money goes back to the payment method you used; depending on your card issuer or bank it may take a few business days to appear.

Once a refund is issued, that license key stops working and the app returns to trial mode. Photos you already edited and files you already exported are unaffected.

Apple handles App Store purchases and refunds. Please request those at reportaproblem.apple.com rather than under this policy.
